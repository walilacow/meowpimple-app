喵喵擠痘痘 使用說明與支援

小盜工作室

怎麼玩：每一關是一張可愛的臉，上面藏著幾顆粉刺和痘痘。放大找到它們，白頭點一下就破，其他的按住畫小圈揉到爆開，再一抹擦乾淨。全部清完就換下一張，沒有時限、不會失敗。

常見問題

痘痘好小，找不到？這是故意的。雙指捏合或雙擊可以放大臉；畫面上方的「剩 N 顆」告訴你還有幾顆；10 秒沒擠到，剩下的會淡淡閃一下。

揉了沒反應？膿皰和囊腫要按在痘痘上原地畫小圈，不要畫到痘痘外面；囊腫要連續揉、中途不要放手。第一次玩的教學可以在「設定 → 再看一次教學」重看。

不想看到血？「設定 → 血腥程度」選「溫和」，囊腫只會流膿不流血。第一次開始遊戲時也會先問你。

沒有聲音或震動？到「設定」確認音效與震動已開；震動只有 iPhone 有，iPad 與 Mac 沒有。

會重複嗎？臉庫有 1,024 張不同的臉，全部遇過一輪才會重新洗牌；同一張臉第二次遇到時難度和痘痘位置都會不一樣。還有一張藏起來的彩蛋。

資料會不會不見？進度、成就、統計都存在這台裝置上；換裝置不會自動同步。

支援的裝置

iPhone、iPad（iOS／iPadOS 17 以上），以及 Apple Silicon 的 Mac（以 iPad App 方式執行）。

問題回報與建議

寫信到 walilacow.hu@gmail.com，請附上 App 版本（在「關於」頁）、裝置型號與系統版本，有截圖更好。

聲明

這是遊戲，不是醫療建議。現實中的粉刺與痘痘請交給皮膚科，不要自己擠。

Meow Pimple Pop Support

小盜工作室 (Little Thief Studio)

How to play: each round is a cute face with a few blemishes hidden on it. Zoom in to find them, tap whiteheads to pop them, press and rub small circles on the others until they burst, then wipe the skin clean. Clear them all to move to the next face. No timer, no failing.

FAQ

The blemishes are tiny, I can't find them. That's on purpose. Pinch or double-tap to zoom in; the counter at the top shows how many are left; after 10 seconds without a pop the remaining ones shimmer briefly.

Rubbing does nothing. Pustules and cysts need small circles right on the blemish, not around it. Cysts need continuous rubbing without lifting your finger. You can replay the tutorial from Settings.

I'd rather not see blood. Settings → Blood level → Gentle: cysts release pus only. The app also asks you the first time you play.

No sound or haptics? Check Settings. Haptics are iPhone-only; iPad and Mac do not vibrate.

Does it repeat? There are 1,024 different faces, reshuffled only after you have seen them all, and the same face gets a different difficulty and layout the next time. There is also one hidden Easter egg.

Will I lose my data? Progress, achievements, and statistics live on this device only and do not sync between devices.

Supported devices

iPhone and iPad (iOS/iPadOS 17 or later), and Apple Silicon Macs (running as an iPad app).

Bug reports and suggestions

Email walilacow.hu@gmail.com with the app version (from the About screen), your device model and OS version, and a screenshot if possible.

Disclaimer

This is a game, not medical advice. Please leave real blemishes to a dermatologist.
