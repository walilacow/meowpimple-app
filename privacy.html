生效日期：2026 年 10 月 11 日

一句話說明：喵喵擠痘痘不收集、不上傳、不分享你的任何個人資料。遊玩紀錄、成就與設定都只存在你自己的裝置裡。

1. 我們不收集資料

本 App 沒有帳號系統、沒有伺服器，不連網，也沒有使用任何廣告、分析、追蹤或當機回報工具。我們（小盜工作室）看不到你在 App 裡的任何內容。

你的遊玩進度（遇過哪些臉、擠了幾顆）、成就、統計與設定（音效、震動、血腥程度等）都只存在你的裝置上。刪除 App 就會一併刪除。

如果你開啟了 iCloud 或電腦備份，這些資料可能會包含在 Apple 的裝置備份中，由 Apple 依照你的設定處理。

3. App 會要求的權限

本 App 不需要任何系統權限：不用相機、麥克風、相簿、定位或通知。觸覺回饋（震動）由系統直接提供，不涉及任何資料。

4. 購買

本 App 為一次付費買斷，透過 Apple 的 App Store 完成；付款與帳號資料由 Apple 處理，我們收不到你的姓名、付款方式或 Apple 帳號資訊。App 內沒有廣告，也沒有額外購買項目。

5. 兒童隱私

本 App 不收集任何人的個人資料，包括 13 歲以下的兒童。

如果之後的版本改變了資料處理方式，我們會先更新這一頁，並在 App 更新說明裡告知。

Meow Pimple Pop Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

In one sentence: Meow Pimple Pop does not collect, upload, or share any of your personal data. Your progress, achievements, and settings stay on your device.

1. We do not collect data

The app has no accounts and no servers, never connects to the internet, and does not use any advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting tools. We (小盜工作室, "Little Thief Studio") cannot see anything you do in the app.

2. Where your data lives

Your play progress (which faces you have met, how many blemishes you have popped), achievements, statistics, and settings (sound, haptics, blood level, and so on) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you use iCloud or computer backups, this data may be included in Apple's device backup and handled by Apple according to your settings.

3. Permissions

The app requests no system permissions: no camera, microphone, photos, location, or notifications. Haptic feedback is provided by the system and involves no data.

4. Purchases

The app is a one-time paid purchase through Apple's App Store. Payment and account details are handled by Apple; we never receive your name, payment method, or Apple ID. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

5. Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this page first and mention it in the app's release notes.
